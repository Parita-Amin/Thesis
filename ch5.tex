\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{chap:conclusion}

\begin{doublespace}

This Chapter presents a summary of the research undertaken and 
contained in this document.
It identifies the contributions made in this thesis and opportunities for future work.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

After motivating the topic in Chapter 1,
Chapter 2 provided a detailed background study on the fundamental topics
supporting \ac{lod} and the tools which facilitate its publication on the web.
These included 
Semantic Web, 
Linked Open Data, 
\ac{rdf}, and 
\ac{owl} ontologies. 
A methodology of transforming data contained in a spreadsheet
into \ac{lod} available on the web of data was described, 
both in general terms (Chapter 3) 
and in the specific case of Hepting's student feedback survey (Chapter 4).
The thesis can be considered as a guide for researchers who are interested in 
transforming their own data into \ac{lod}.
In response to the question asked about whether this next web of data is sufficiently attractive to use,
I have found sufficiency with opportunities for improvement.

\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:futurework}

While the vocabulary developed for Hepting's survey is a first step in describing
Likert items using Likert scales, this vocabulary could be improved by reusing parts of
other existing vocabularies. Adding expressiveness to the vocabulary by modelling a range of Likert~\cite{south2022effective} scales with different labels and values would also be valuable.

OpenRefine was used in this thesis for mapping data to RDF.
Although it has a solid user interface, RMLMapper may be preferred in
future because it permits the mappings to be scripted and executed from
a command line interface. Such an approach may be preferrable for the same
mapping applied to several spreadsheets.

Hepting has a substantial collection of feedback data from these surveys spanning
several years. Conversion of all this data into \ac{lod}, in a way that permits
effective querying and analysis, would be an important achievement. The latest
survey collections have once again been done on paper, and it would be helpful
to have a means to support data entry.
