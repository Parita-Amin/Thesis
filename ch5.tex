\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{chap:conclusion}

\begin{doublespace}

This Chapter presents a summary of the research undertaken and 
contained in this document.
It identifies the contributions made in this thesis and opportunities for future work.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

After motivating the topic in Chapter 1,
Chapter 2 provides a detailed background study on the fundamental topics
supporting \ac{lod} and the tools which facilitate its publication on the web.
These include 
Semantic Web, 
Linked Open Data, 
\ac{rdf}, and 
\ac{owl} ontologies. 
This thesis describes the methodology of transforming data contained in a spreadsheet
into \ac{lod} that is available on the web of data, 
both in general terms (Chapter 3) 
and in the specific case of Dr. Hepting's student feedback survey (Chapter 4).
The thesis can be considered as a guide for researchers who are interested in 
transforming their own data into \ac{lod}.

\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:futurework}

\begin{itemize}

	\item The FOOPS Evaluator shows that 30\% of the {\tt Survey} vocabulary 
	obeys the FAIR principles. 
	The results show that the vocabulary 
	isn't using existing vocabularies to describe its terms. 
	The survey vocabulary can be redefined using existing vocabularies.
	
	\item Using RMLConversion by employing 
	RMLMapper or RMLStreamer to map data into \ac{rdf}.
	
	\item Modelling the range of Likert~\cite{south2022effective} scales 
	with different labels and values.
	
	\item Jekyll-RDF integration.
	
	\item Conversion and management of all feedback data collected by Hepting.
	
	\item Developing a data entry form for the feedback survey instrument 
	using Schimatos\footnote{\tt https://github.com/schimatos/schimatos.org} 
	(a \ac{shacl}-based web-form generator).
	
	\item Versioning of the survey ontology is a topic of interest. 
	The ontology vocabularies can be reused for appropriate applications 
	but they may need to be updated over time. Hence, 
	ontology versioning and version management of the survey instrument 
	for this feedback survey, 
	as well as for other types of surveys is an aspect of this research that can be explored.
	
\end{itemize}
